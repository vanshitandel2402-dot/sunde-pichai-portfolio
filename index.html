About Sundar Pichai

Sundar Pichai is a renowned technology leader and the Chief Executive Officer of Google and Alphabet. He was born on July 12, 1972, in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, India. He studied Metallurgical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur and later pursued advanced studies in the United States. Pichai joined Google in 2004 and played an important role in the development and growth of several major Google products. He became widely known for his leadership in products such as Google Chrome and Chrome OS. In 2015, he became the CEO of Google, and in 2019, he also became the CEO of Alphabet. Under his leadership, Google has continued to focus on artificial intelligence, cloud computing, search technology, and other innovative technologies. Sundar Pichai is admired for his calm leadership, technical knowledge, and ability to guide large teams. His journey from India to leading one of the world's most influential technology companies is an inspiration to many students and aspiring engineers.
